Free and local: no restrictions on your own PC

The free route is our desktop app, Locally Uncensored. It runs open models on your own hardware, on Windows, macOS on Apple Silicon and Linux. It is open source under AGPL-3.0. There is no account and no credits, and nothing leaves your machine.

The trade is your hardware. What you can run depends on your graphics card, and the largest models do not fit on a home PC. That gap is what the hosted side is for. Many people use both: local for private work, the cloud for the big models or for a phone.

What stays blocked everywhere

On LU Cloud two lines never move, at every content policy setting and on every model:

  • Material involving minors. Refused on every request, with no exception.
  • Photographs of real, identifiable people uploaded without their consent. Refused on every request as well.

The Terms of Service apply on top of that. On your own PC the law where you live applies, as it does to anything else you make.

What unrestricted does not mean

The word promises a lot, so here is where it stops.

  • Not every model answers everything. No extra filter from us does not change how a model was trained. A model that was built to decline still declines. The No Filter mark shows which chat models answered in our test.
  • Not a jailbreak. There is no hidden filter to trick, so there is no prompt to maintain. If a model refuses, switch the model.
  • Not free on the hosted side. There is no free hosted tier. Credit packs start at 15 EUR for 450,000 credits, with no subscription, and the credits do not expire. Plans are Hosted at 19 EUR, Pro at 49 EUR and Max at 99 EUR a month.
  • Not anonymous. LU Cloud needs an account at lu-labs.ai, and a hosted request leaves your machine. If that is a problem, use the desktop app.
  • Not a free pass. Illegal content stays illegal, whichever tool made it.

Questions people ask

Is there a free unrestricted AI?

Yes, if it runs on your own PC. The Locally Uncensored desktop app is free and open source under AGPL-3.0, for Windows, macOS on Apple Silicon and Linux, and it runs open models with no filter from us. The hosted side has no free tier. LU Cloud starts with a credit pack from 15 EUR for 450,000 credits, with no subscription, and the credits do not expire.

Is unrestricted AI legal?

The question is the use, not the tool. Illegal content stays illegal, whichever model produced it, and the law where you live applies. On LU Cloud the Terms of Service apply as well, and two things are refused on every request at every setting: material involving minors, and photographs of real, identifiable people uploaded without their consent.

What is the difference between unfiltered AI and a jailbreak?

A jailbreak is a prompt that tries to talk a filtered model past its filter. It stops working when the vendor changes the filter. Unfiltered AI here means LU adds no extra filter to hosted text, so there is nothing to argue with. What remains is the model's own training, and models differ. 13 of the 36 cloud chat models we measured answer in full without refusing, and only those carry the No Filter mark.

Does unrestricted AI chat work on my phone?

Yes. LU Cloud runs in the browser, so chat, images and video work on a phone without installing anything. The free desktop app runs on a computer with Windows, macOS on Apple Silicon or Linux.

What is blocked anyway?

On LU Cloud, at every setting and on every model: material involving minors, and photographs of real, identifiable people uploaded without their consent. Both are refused on every request, and the Terms of Service apply. For images and video, your content policy setting also decides what renders. Standard, the default, refuses some prompts.

Do I need an account?

For LU Cloud, yes. Credits belong to an account at lu-labs.ai. A subscription is not needed, because a credit pack is a single purchase. The desktop app needs no account at all.

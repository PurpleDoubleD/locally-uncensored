Remote Access

Use Locally Uncensored on your own PC from your phone's browser, over your home Wi-Fi or from anywhere through a Cloudflare tunnel. The PC does the work; the phone sends your messages and shows the answers.

What remote access does

The desktop app starts a small web server on your PC, and your phone opens it in its normal web browser. There is nothing to install on the phone, and in version 3.0.6 there is no separate phone app: the phone side is this web page. A browser on a second computer works the same way.

On the phone page you chat with the model on your PC, can pick another model, attach pictures and switch on "Agent". What the agent may touch on your PC is decided by four switches on the desktop, and all four start off. The step by step chapter Remote access, step by step walks through every screen.

Two ways to connect, both started by you:

  • LAN: phone and PC in the same Wi-Fi. The traffic stays in your network.
  • Internet: LU starts a Cloudflare tunnel and gets a public https address. Nothing to set up on your router.

1. Over your own Wi-Fi (LAN)

1Open the "Remote" tab in the sidebar

It sits next to "Chat" and "Code". A chat model must be picked, otherwise "Dispatch" stays greyed out.

2Press "Dispatch", then "LAN"

Pick the folder the agent may work in. Cancel the dialog and nothing starts. Your home folder or a system folder is refused.

3Scan the QR code

A green "LIVE" panel shows a QR code, a six-digit passcode and an address like http://192.168.1.20:11435/mobile. Scan the QR code with the phone's camera, or type the address into the phone's browser.

4Type the passcode

The phone asks for the "Access Code". Type the six digits from the desktop and tap "Connect". The QR code does not contain the passcode, so you always type it.

2. From anywhere (Internet)

Press "Dispatch", then "Internet", then pick the folder. The panel shows "Connecting to Cloudflare…" and holds the QR code back until the public address works; LU waits up to 15 seconds. The address ends in trycloudflare.com/mobile, is new for every session and exists only while it runs. Scan and type the passcode as above. Right after scanning the first connection can take 5 to 10 seconds; if the phone shows a DNS error, wait a moment and reload.

You do not need to install anything for this. The helper program cloudflared is downloaded by LU itself the first time, from Cloudflare's releases on GitHub, and fetched again when the copy is more than 30 days old.

3. The six-digit passcode

  • It is valid for 15 minutes; a countdown shows the time left, and LU makes a new one when it runs out.
  • The circular arrow next to it makes a new one at once. Phones that are already paired stay connected.
  • It is only for pairing. After "Connect" the phone stays signed in for an hour, extended while you use it, for at most 24 hours. Then it pairs again.
  • Three wrong codes in a row lock out new tries for 60 seconds.

Anyone who can reach the address and knows the current passcode can pair, so do not post a photo of the panel. If someone may have seen it, make a new passcode and remove unknown devices.

4. The four permission switches

They sit in the desktop app under Settings, Voice & Remote, Remote Access, "Permissions". They apply to every paired device together, not per device. All four are off until you turn them on, and off again each time LU starts. The phone can see them but not change them.

SwitchWhat it allows
Filesystem AccessThe agent may list, read, search and write files in the folder you picked at Dispatch, and take a screenshot of the PC.
Downloads & InstallsModel downloads and installs on the PC.
Process ControlStart and stop the local engines, make pictures through the PC, list running programs.
Shell & Code ExecutionRun commands and code on the PC as your user. The app itself calls it risky: leave it off unless you trust the network.

With all four off the phone can still chat, and the agent can still search the web, fetch pages and read the time and basic system information.

5. Connected devices, Stop and Restart

  • Connected Devices under the same settings lists every paired device with its address and browser. The trash icon ("Disconnect") throws one out at once. There is no renaming and no per-device setting.
  • Stop on the green panel ends the session: the server stops, the tunnel closes, the phone can no longer reach the PC.
  • Restart starts the server again for the same chat with a new passcode. Every phone pairs again. Restart does not bring back an internet tunnel; for a new internet address press "Stop" and dispatch again with "Internet".
  • The PC must be on, awake and running LU. LU does not keep the computer awake, and quitting LU ends the session. Closing the window with its X only hides LU to the tray, so the server keeps running.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCause and fix
"Dispatch" is greyed outNo chat model picked. Pick or install one.
Phone cannot open the address (LAN)The phone is on mobile data, a guest network or a different Wi-Fi. Put it on the same network as the PC. Otherwise use Internet.
"Connection failed" in the same Wi-FiWindows: the firewall. LU adds the rule "LU Remote 11435" for private networks only when it runs with administrator rights; otherwise allow LU once in the Windows firewall and make sure your Wi-Fi counts as a private network.
Linux: allow port 11435 in your firewall if one is active.
"Invalid access code"Typo, or the code changed (after 15 minutes, after the circular arrow, after Restart). Type the code the desktop shows now.
"Too many attempts. Try again in (N)s"Three wrong codes. Wait, then type the current code.
"…no public URL appeared within 15 s…"cloudflared cannot reach Cloudflare, usually a firewall or VPN. LAN still works. Fix it, then press "Stop" and dispatch again with "Internet".
"Could not bind 0.0.0.0:11435"Port 11435 is taken, usually by a second LU or an earlier session. Press "Stop", quit other LU instances from the tray, dispatch again.
The phone suddenly asks for the passcodeRestart was pressed, the device was disconnected, or 24 hours passed. Pair again.
The agent on the phone says a tool needs a permissionTurn that switch on in the desktop app under Settings, Voice & Remote, Remote Access.

Privacy notes

  • Compute stays on your PC. Models and tool calls run on the PC. Only the chat travels between the PC and the paired device.
  • LAN is plain http. Inside your network the connection is not encrypted. Use LAN only on a network you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi, not on a public hotspot.
  • In Internet mode Cloudflare relays the traffic. It is encrypted between each device and Cloudflare, not end to end, so Cloudflare could read it.
  • No analytics kit, no crash reports. On its own the app checks GitHub for updates and counts presses of the Cloud switch. Settings, General, Privacy lists both.
  • You are in control. Press "Stop" any time; a new session needs a new pairing.

Uncensored AI chat online, no filter on text

We build Locally Uncensored, the free open source AI studio that runs models on your own machine. Plenty of people who find it do not have a 24 GB card, and some just want a chat window that works from a phone or a work laptop. That is what the hosted layer at LU Labs is for. Same team, same catalog philosophy, rented GPUs instead of yours.

The short version of the promise, so nobody has to guess: hosted text models answer the way they were trained, and we do not put a moderation layer on top of them. That claim is narrow on purpose. Read the limits section before you pay for anything.

What you get

  • 46 chat models in one picker, and every plan and every credit pack reaches all of them. DeepSeek V3.2 is the cheap everyday default at 0.038 credits per output token. Kimi K3 is the one you switch to for a hard prompt, and it reads images on a 1M token context. Euryale 70B, Lunaris 8B and MythoMax 13B are the community fine tunes people pick for character work. GLM 5.3 Flash takes images too and reasons on every turn. Gemma 4 26B and gpt-oss 120B round out the cheap end. Qwen 3.8 Max and Qwen 3.8 A95B sit in the same list at 0.4951 and 0.6 credits per output token.
  • Tool calls on every one of them. A prompt transport carries them, so the Agent and the coding agent work even on models with no native tool API.
  • An image and video lane too, with 10 image models, 5 video models, inpainting, background removal, an eraser and upscaling to 2k, 4k or 8k. Different rules apply there, see below.
  • Your own API key if you would rather use SillyTavern, Aider, LibreChat or curl.
  • No training on your data, never sold, with accounts and billing in the EU.

How to start

  1. Open lu-labs.ai and sign in. Nothing to install, it runs in the browser, including on a Mac and on a phone.
  2. Buy a credit pack or the Hosted plan. Any pack is available from your first purchase.
  3. Open chat, pick a model, type. If you are unsure, start on DeepSeek V3.2.
  4. Optional: on Windows or Linux, install the free desktop app and flip the Cloud switch. It signs into the same account, so local models and hosted models share one picker.
  5. Optional: create a key under Settings, Cloud API keys. The base URL is https://lu-labs.ai/api/inference/v1 in the OpenAI chat completions shape. The key is shown once.

The step by step version, one setting at a time, lives in the full setup guide.

The honest limits

  • Text: no moderation layer from us. The model still answers as it was trained, so a fine tune built to decline will decline. Choosing the model is the real control you have.
  • Image and video: these follow the Terms of Service and the prompt is checked before anything renders. We do not describe them the way we describe text.
  • Always refused, everywhere: anything involving minors. No exception, no lane, no model.
  • Shared hardware. Managed H100, A100 and B200 class GPUs, which means a short queue at busy hours.
  • Curated catalog. You cannot upload your own checkpoint.
  • It leaves your machine. That is what hosted means. If it is not acceptable for your work, run the free local app instead and keep everything on your disk.

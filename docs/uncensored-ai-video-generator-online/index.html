Image in, five seconds out: how the workflow runs

  1. Open lu-labs.ai and sign in. It runs in the browser, so a Mac or a phone works as well as a PC.
  2. Buy a credit pack or a Hosted plan. Packs start at 15 EUR and need no subscription.
  3. Go to Settings, General, Content policy and move the setting off its default. Signed out, that section only offers you a sign-in link.
  4. Get your input image. Either upload one you already have, or generate it in LU first on one of the seven image models that render adult content. Generating it in LU spends credits from the same wallet, from 1,000 credits an image on Neta Lumina (anime) Spicy, Z-Image Base and Nucleus up to 2,500 on Wan 2.2 Realism, and that is on top of the clip price.
  5. Open the Animate lane, hand over the image, pick a model from the table above and write what should happen in the frame.
  6. Start the job. What comes back is five seconds long. Download it, because hosted renders are held for seven days and then removed.
  7. Optional: run Wan 2.2 Spicy Extend on the result for another 15,000 credits to make it longer.

Step four surprises people, so plainly: the clip price does not include the input image. Make that image in LU and you pay twice out of one balance, so a session of ten stills and three clips costs more than the video table alone suggests. Exact image quantities are on the image generation page.

How the content policy setting works

Hosted image and video generation follows a setting that lives in your account, not a switch we flip for everybody. Settings, General, Content policy has three options, and the line above them reads "Applies to images and video rendered in the cloud. Text is unaffected, and nothing on your own machine is." Strict is described in the app as "The tightest filter we have. Choose this if others use your screen." Standard is "The default for every account." Off is "No filter beyond the legal limits below."

Those legal limits are printed under all three options and they do not move: "Material involving minors, and photographs of real people uploaded without their consent, are refused on every request whatever this is set to, and reported." Content involving minors is blocked everywhere, always, on every model, with no exception and no setting that changes it. A photograph of a real, identifiable person needs that person's consent before you upload it. The Terms of Service apply on top of both.

We describe hosted text differently, because it works differently: on the 47 chat models we add no moderation layer of our own. Video is not text and we are not going to call it filterless. The account setting is the honest description, and the full walkthrough of it is in the cloud handbook chapter.

The honest limits

  • Clips are five seconds. Extending one is a separate paid run.
  • The adult-capable models have no text-to-video path. If you have no picture, you have to make one first, and that costs credits too. Wan 3.0 Spicy and Wan 3.0 Prime Spicy can take a second picture as the end frame, which is another image to find or pay for.
  • Hosted renders are kept for seven days, then removed. Download what you want to keep.
  • Catalog prices change. The numbers on this page were checked on September 22, 2026, and the machine-readable copy lives at cloud-facts.json.
  • A failed job can still cost you, and quoted maximums assume every run succeeds. Real months land under the table figures.
  • Media jobs are limited to 30 requests a minute per account, so a batch queues rather than all starting at once.
  • A hosted request leaves your machine. That is what hosted means, and no phrasing changes it. If it is unacceptable for your work, the local app is the answer.
  • The catalog is curated. You cannot upload your own video checkpoint to our GPUs.

FAQ

Is the video generator uncensored?

The adult-capable video models appear once you change the content policy setting in your own account. The default setting is Standard, and on Standard a hardcore prompt is refused. We do not describe hosted video the way we describe hosted text, where we add no moderation layer at all. Two lines never move at any setting: material involving minors, and photographs of real identifiable people uploaded without their consent.

Can I type a prompt and get a video, with no image?

Not on the adult-capable models. Every one of them is image-to-video only, so the render needs a still to start from. LU has text-to-video models in the catalog, such as Wan 2.2 720p and LTX 2.3, but those are not the adult-capable ones.

How long are the clips?

Five seconds. Wan 2.2 Spicy Extend adds length to a clip you already rendered, and it costs 15,000 credits each time you run it.

Does it do NSFW video?

Yes, on the fourteen adult-capable models listed above, and only after you set your account content policy to Off. All fourteen render a clip from an image you supply. Wan 2.2 Spicy Extend is a separate tool that lengthens a clip you already made, and it is not one of the fourteen.

Can I animate a photo of a real person?

Only with that person's consent. Uploading a photograph of a real, identifiable person without their consent is refused on every request whatever the content policy is set to, and reported.

Do you keep the clips?

Cloud renders are held for seven days, then they are gone. Download anything you want to keep. There is no training on user data and it is never sold, and accounts and billing are in the EU.

Does it run on a Mac or a phone?

Yes, in the browser at lu-labs.ai. The free desktop app is Windows and Linux only, so on a Mac or a phone the hosted studio is the route.

What is blocked no matter what I set?

Material involving minors, on every request, on every model, with no exception. Photographs of real people uploaded without their consent, the same way. The Terms of Service apply on top of both.

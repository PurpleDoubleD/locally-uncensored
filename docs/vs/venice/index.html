Venice AI alternatives in 2026: eight honest picks

Alternatives to Venice AI fall into three groups: studios covering chat, images and video from one balance, text only inference hosts with large model lists, and specialists for images or roleplay. Eight are below, LU Cloud first and then seven we do not run, with the published price, the date we read it and what each lacks. If you are still working out what uncensored AI covers before you compare prices, that page defines it first.

What Venice publishes today

Read on venice.ai/pricing on September 22, 2026: Free at 0 USD with 10 text and 15 image prompts a day, Pro at 18 USD a month with what the page calls "unlimited" text prompts, 1,000 image prompts a day on Venice models and 100 credits, Pro Plus at 68 USD with 7,500 credits, Max at 200 USD with 22,500. The page states that 100 credits equal 1 USD and lists NSFW image generation as its own row. Venice is based in Sheridan, Wyoming; its download page offered iPhone, iPad, Android and an APK on September 9, 2026, no desktop app.

Trustpilot showed 1.9 out of 5 from 55 reviews on September 9, 2026 (trustpilot.com). Reviews we read that day included complaints about the size of the Pro credit allowance. The Terms of Service, read the same day, forbid submissions that are "objectionable, profane, indecent, pornographic".

Eight Venice AI alternatives

1. LU Cloud, for chat plus adult-capable image and video in one wallet

For text, pictures and clips billed from a single balance, with a free offline route on the same account. Hosted is 19 EUR a month with 900,000 credits, Pro 49 EUR with 2,350,000 and Max 99 EUR with 5,000,000, read on our pricing page on September 22, 2026. Packs need no subscription and do not expire, from 15 EUR for 450,000 credits to 300 EUR for 9,750,000. All 47 chat models open on every plan and pack with no moderation layer from us on text; 24 of the 46 we measured answered without refusing on September 10, 2026.

Image and video carry a published price per run. Seven image models render adult work, from 1,000 credits an image on Neta Lumina Spicy, Z-Image Base and Nucleus, up to 900 from one Hosted wallet, to 2,500 on Wan 2.2 Realism. Fourteen video models do the same, all of them image-to-video only in five second clips with the input image not included: LTX 2.3 Spicy 10,000 credits a clip, up to 90 on Hosted; Seedance 2.5 Spicy 90,000, up to 10. Each maximum spends the whole wallet on one model. What is missing: no free hosted tier, no mobile app, no crypto, desktop on Windows and Linux only. Adult image and video output appears only after you change the content policy setting in your account, material involving minors is refused everywhere, and a photo of a real person needs consent.

2. Featherless.ai, for the widest model list

For reaching one specific fine tune from Hugging Face, including abliterated builds, which the model page lists as a filter category. The Chat plan is 25 USD a month with context up to 32K and 4 concurrent units, Developer 50 USD a month of credit billed per token, Business on request, read at featherless.ai on September 9, 2026. What is missing: no image and no video models, no free tier, and the Chat plan is for human driven use rather than API traffic. More in the Featherless comparison.

3. NanoGPT, for paying per request and in crypto

NanoGPT is built for people who want no subscription. Deposits start at 0.10 USD in crypto or 1 USD by card and usage comes off that balance, with an optional subscription listed at 12 USD a month, read on nano-gpt.com/pricing on September 9, 2026. Their docs list 17 coins including Monero, Bitcoin and Zcash, and their payment post of September 1, 2026 puts Monero at 38.55 percent of their August 2026 crypto deposit volume. What is missing: what the 12 USD subscription includes was not in the page text we received, we found no hard no-logging commitment and no documented app, and nobody has narrowed the 602 text models down for you.

4. Sogni.ai, for browser image generation

For image work with a wide checkpoint selection. The site advertises "50+ NSFW-capable models" and "Unlimited plans from $20/month", read on September 22, 2026. What is missing: beyond those two lines we have nothing checked, so treat chat, video, API, payment and privacy as unchecked rather than missing.

5. Mage.space, for subscription image and video

Mage sells LoRA workflows and custom model imports. Basic is 10 USD a month, Pro 30 USD, Pro Plus 60 USD and Max 200 USD, with a 0 USD membership row also shown, read on mage.space/pricing on September 9, 2026. Basic includes 500 monthly gems, Pro 1,500 plus what the page lists as "unlimited video generation", Max 15,000. The site names Stable Diffusion 1.5, SDXL, Flux, Flux 2, Chroma, Krea 2, Z-Image, Wan Video and Qwen. What is missing: no chat product, and payment, privacy, API and apps were not on the pages we read.

6. Civitai, for the community model ecosystem

For the checkpoints and LoRAs themselves rather than a fixed catalog. Bronze is 10 USD a month with 10,000 Green Buzz, Silver 25 USD with 25,000 and Gold 50 USD with 50,000, read on civitai.com/pricing on September 9, 2026. Card payments stopped on May 23, 2025 and crypto is now a direct wallet deposit, with BNB, BTC, DOGE, ETH, LTC, PYUSD, SHIB, SOL, TRX, USDC and USDT named on their crypto guide when we read it on September 9, 2026. What is missing: no chat models, and no Buzz cost per image, so cost per picture cannot be worked out. Their safety page, read the same day, says "Content that reproduces the likeness of real people, in any context, is prohibited".

7. SpicyChat, for character roleplay

SpicyChat publishes a model table per tier. Free is 0, Get a Taste 5.95 EUR a month, True Supporter 19.95 EUR and I'm All In 29.95 EUR, read on spicychat.ai on September 9, 2026, after a price increase effective September 8, 2026. Fifteen models are published with the tier that unlocks each, Euryale 70B among them. What is missing: context is small and the vendor's pages disagree, the docs giving 4,096 tokens on Free and up to 16,384 on the top plan while the sales page advertises 32K. One image model is documented, DreamShaper-8, and there is no video. Their own notice says the iOS app was removed from the App Store, with access ending on September 25, 2025.

8. Backyard AI, for roleplay with a free local mode

For heavy roleplay chat volume. The free tier gives 300 messages a week with 16,384 tokens of context on one model, Standard is 12 USD a month with 6 models and Pro 35 USD a month with 11 models and up to 100k context, read on backyard.ai/cloud on September 9, 2026. Named Pro models include Llama 3.3 Euryale v2.3 70B and Magnum 72B, and local running is free. What is missing: no image and no video generation on the cloud page, and no OpenAI compatible API key. More in the Backyard AI comparison.

When Venice is the better pick

Venice has a 0 USD entry and we do not. Ten text and fifteen image prompts a day is enough to decide whether it suits you before a card is involved.

If your use is mostly typing, Pro at 18 USD with what their page calls "unlimited" text prompts on Venice hosted models is a simpler bill than a credit meter. Venice also ships iOS and Android apps, lists 120 LLMs including closed frontier models we do not sell, and takes crypto.

When LU is the better pick

One wallet covers chat, image and video, and the same balance pays for API keys, so there is no app allowance to track beside a metered one.

Seven image models and fourteen image-to-video models render adult content, and each carries a published credit price per image or per five second clip, so you can work out the monthly ceiling before you pay. They appear once you change the content policy setting in your account, and material involving minors stays refused everywhere.

The desktop app for Windows and Linux is free under AGPL-3.0 and runs supported models on your own hardware without credits, on the same account as the hosted catalog. The API is OpenAI compatible at the base URL https://lu-labs.ai/api/inference/v1, so SillyTavern, Aider, LibreChat and curl connect without a wrapper; the uncensored AI API page covers how keys are created and revoked. Billing is in the EU and every price is in EUR.

Questions people ask

It depends which part of Venice you use. For chat, image and video from one balance, LU Cloud. For text only with a very large model list, Featherless.ai. For pay as you go with crypto, NanoGPT. For images in the browser, Sogni.ai, Mage.space or Civitai.

What does Venice AI cost?

Read on venice.ai/pricing on September 22, 2026: Free 0 USD with 10 text and 15 image prompts a day, Pro 18 USD a month with what the page calls "unlimited" text prompts, 1,000 image prompts a day on Venice models and 100 credits, Pro Plus 68 USD with 7,500 credits, Max 200 USD with 22,500 credits.

Does Venice AI have a desktop app?

No. The Venice download page offered iPhone, iPad, Android and a direct APK when it was read on September 9, 2026. LU ships a desktop app for Windows and Linux under AGPL-3.0 that also runs models locally without credits.

We have not reconciled the two documents, so read both before you pay.

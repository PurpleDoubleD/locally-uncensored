LU on Android

The short version: the Android app is here as an APK with the 3.0.7 release. The iPhone app is in review at the App Store, and Google Play follows in the next days to two weeks. You do not have to wait: download the APK now.

Download for Android

LU-3.0.7-Android.apk, about 27 MB, for Android 7.0 or newer on a 64-bit phone (arm64).

What the App Does

  • Run AI models on your phone: download a model once, then chat offline. Chats with on-device models never leave the phone.
  • Ask questions about your own documents, with memory and personas.
  • LU Cloud with your account: larger hosted chat models, Create for images and video, and dictation.
  • Connect to LU on your own computer over your home Wi-Fi and chat with the models on your PC from the phone.
  • Import your own model files and use the full model catalogue.

How to Install

  1. Open the download on your phone.
  2. Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Allow "Install unknown apps" for it.
  3. Tap Install.

Already have 3.0.7-pre1 on the phone? The APK installs over it and keeps your data.

Updates

The app does not update itself and does not announce a new version. To update, download the newer APK and install it over the old one. It carries the same signature, so your data stays.

When the Google Play Version Comes Out

The version on Google Play is signed differently, so it cannot install over the APK. Before you switch, export your chats in the app (Settings, Data), uninstall the APK, then install from Google Play and bring your chats back in.

Your PC as the Engine

On your home Wi-Fi the app connects to Locally Uncensored on your own PC, which does the compute. The desktop side is on the Remote Access page.

No PC at home? Use the browser

The hosted side opens in a browser, which is also how it works on a phone. It reaches 48 chat models online, paid from credit packs that start at 15 EUR and need no subscription. See what it costs.

MiniMax H3 Uncensored Video in LU Cloud: Chili, Enhanced, Hyper and Max Compared

MiniMax H3 now comes to LU Cloud as a family of unfiltered video models: Chili, Enhanced, Hyper and Max, plus Max Turbo. They make video from a text prompt, from a first frame (and optionally a last frame), or from up to nine reference pictures and a reference sound. The sound track is generated together with the picture. Within the content setting of your account they render what you describe, without a second filter on top. They run in LU Cloud: right now in the browser at lu-labs.ai and in the LU phone app. The Locally Uncensored desktop app shows them from version 3.1.4 on.

Short version

Clips from 4 or 5 up to 15 seconds. Max: 480p, 768p or 1080p. Enhanced: 768p or 2K. Hyper and Chili: 768p. Text to video, image to video with first and last frame, and reference to video with up to nine images and an audio clip up to 30 seconds. Sound is always on. From 10,000 credits per clip.

"From" means the shortest clip at the smallest resolution. A longer or sharper clip costs more: H3 Max at 1080p and 5 seconds is 72,000 credits. Hyper and Chili do not scale in a straight line with length, especially in reference mode: a 15 second Hyper reference clip is 213,000 credits. The studio shows the price of your exact settings before you start.

Max Turbo is the cheapest way to 1080p. Enhanced is the only one that goes to 2K, and it has no image to video mode. Hyper and Chili render at 768p and start at 4 seconds; Hyper is the cheapest for a short clip from text.

The Three Ways to Start a Clip

  • Text to video: describe scene, action, camera and sound. Aspect ratios 16:9, 21:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4 and 9:16.
  • Image to video: a first frame decides the canvas; an optional last frame makes the clip move from one to the other.
  • Reference to video: up to nine pictures, each at least 256 pixels on the short side, named in the prompt as @Image1, @Image2 and so on, and an optional MP3 or WAV of up to 30 seconds as @Audio1.

What Unfiltered Means Here

These models follow the content setting of your account instead of an extra filter, so the choice of what your own clips show is yours, and we do not train on your prompts or clips and do not sell them. Content involving minors is refused on every model, and the prompt is checked before rendering. Unfiltered does not mean every clip comes out as written.

How to Use It

  1. Sign in at lu-labs.ai, or in the LU phone app, with your LU Cloud account, and open the video studio.
  2. Pick a MiniMax H3 model and the mode: text, image or reference.
  3. Choose resolution, aspect ratio and length, check the price shown, and generate.

In the desktop app the models appear with version 3.1.4. Until then, the browser works with the same account and credits.

Limits

  • Hosted: prompts, pictures and audio leave your machine. For fully private work, generate videos locally in the free desktop app.
  • Longer and sharper clips cost more credits.
  • Content involving minors is refused on every model.

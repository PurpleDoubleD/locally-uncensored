GLM 5.3 Flash Uncensored Online: GLM 5.3 Flash Dealigned in LU Cloud

GLM 5.3 Flash Dealigned is in LU Cloud as of November 24, 2026. It is GLM 5.3 Flash with the refusals removed in the weights by the team dealignai, served in fp8. It keeps the parts that make GLM 5.3 Flash practical: a context of about one million tokens, image input, and native tool calls for Agent mode and the Code tab. You use it in Locally Uncensored through LU Cloud, in the desktop app, in the browser and on your phone, with no graphics card of your own.

Short version

GLM 5.3 Flash with the refusals taken out of the weights. 1,048,576 tokens of context, up to 131,072 out. It always thinks before it answers, effort Low, Medium or High. Reads images, up to ten per request. Native tool calls. 11,250 credits per million input tokens and 37,500 per million output. Every plan and every credit pack reaches it. From December 1 it also answers for Gemma 4 No Filter.

What the Model Is

The base is GLM 5.3 in its Flash size, the fast member of the family. dealignai, the team behind the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash build already in LU Cloud, changed the weights so the model stops producing its trained refusals. The change is in the weights themselves. Our host flagged the build on November 23, and we measured it the next day before it went into the catalog.

Removing refusals changes how a model answers, not what it knows. A model built this way can still decline, hedge or get facts wrong, and we do not promise that it never refuses. What it does mean: the choice of model is yours. One rule has no exception on any model: content involving minors is refused.

What We Measured in LU Cloud

We ran it against the live endpoint with our own server key on November 24, 2026:

  • Reasoning: on for every answer. No signal turns it off: with thinking set to none it still reasoned for 23 tokens, with thinking disabled for 28, and with High for 44 on the same riddle. So the Think switch stays on for this model, the way it does for the stock GLM 5.3 Flash.
  • Effort: Low, Medium and High all answer. High is the default.
  • Tools: native tool calls, also with 200 tool definitions in one request. Agent mode and the Code tab run on it.
  • Images: it reads them. A red and a blue test square were both named correctly. Up to ten images per request; an eleventh is rejected.
  • Context: 1,048,576 tokens in, up to 131,072 out.

How to Use It

In the desktop app

In Locally Uncensored on Windows, Mac or Linux, sign in under Settings, General, LU Cloud Account and switch the header to Cloud. Open the model picker: GLM 5.3 Flash Dealigned is in the Uncensored group. It works in Chat, in Agent mode and in the Code tab. The list comes from the server, so no app update is needed.

In the browser

Sign in at lu-labs.ai with the same account. In the browser picker it stands in the Unrestricted group. Same model, same price.

On your phone

The LU app for Android reaches LU Cloud with your account. On iPhone the browser works in Safari until the app is in the App Store.

From your own code

A personal API key reaches it too, unless the key's content setting is strict. A strict key does not reach the models of this group.

What It Costs

11,250 credits per million input tokens and 37,500 per million output tokens. Every plan and every credit pack reaches it.

Two things to keep in mind. Thinking tokens count as output, and this model always thinks, so short questions cost a little more than on a model you can switch to silent. And it is not part of the daily Flash allowance: the stock GLM 5.3 Flash is included on Pro and Max, this build always draws credits.

GLM 5.3 Flash Dealigned or DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Dealigned?

Both come from dealignai, both have a 1M window, read up to ten images and call tools. The differences:

What Happens to Gemma 4 No Filter

Our host removes the Gemma 4 build on December 1, 2026 and names GLM 5.3 Flash Dealigned as its replacement. From that day the Gemma 4 entry is answered, billed and named as GLM 5.3 Flash Dealigned. A saved pick keeps working, and nothing needs to change on your side. You get a larger window, 1M instead of 256K, at almost the same price, and the model now thinks before it answers.

Limits

  • It is new. How it holds up when many people use it at once is not measured yet.
  • It runs on one route. When that route is down, a request is refused with a plain sentence instead of being sent to another model.
  • We have not run it through the test behind the No Filter mark. Its place in the Uncensored group rests on what its makers release it for.
  • Your account's content policy still applies, and material involving minors is refused on every request.
  • Privacy: a hosted request leaves your machine. We do not train on your prompts and do not sell them. If nothing may leave your computer, run a local model in the free desktop app instead.

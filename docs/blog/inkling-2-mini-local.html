Inkling 2 Mini Locally: Which Size Fits Your Card, and How It Compares to Qwen 3.8 27B

Inkling 2 Mini is an open-weight model from Thinking Machines, released on 20 November 2026. It has 26.4B parameters, reads text and images, calls tools and can think before it answers. Locally Uncensored 3.1.3 offers it in 23 GGUF sizes, from a 9.6 GB file to the 48.6 GB full-precision one.

The short answer: with 16 GB of graphics memory take Q3_K_S, with 20 GB take Q4_K_M. Under 13 GB, pick a smaller model.

What Is Different About It

The model has 24 layers, and the middle 12 run three times with shared weights. That gives 48 layer passes per token from 24 layers of weights. Each token also uses only 8 of 128 experts in a layer, so just 3.4B parameters are active, or 5.4B once the extra passes are counted. That is why a 26B model can keep up with denser models of the same size.

Thinking Machines recommends 32 GB or more for its own 20 GB checkpoint. The quantized GGUF files are how it fits on smaller cards, and the lower sizes give up some quality. We have not measured how much.

What we ran

A small test, not a benchmark: Q3_K_S on Windows with an RTX 5080 gave 28.4 to 29.1 tokens per second in the app's benchmark. Chat, thinking, an image and an agent run with file tools all worked. With a 16K context the model loaded without image input, and the app said so.

Without a GPU: Inkling 2 Mini and Small in LU Cloud

Since November 24, 2026 LU Cloud also runs Inkling 2 Mini and its larger sibling Inkling 2 Small, hosted, so they work in the browser, on Android and on any computer without a big graphics card. Both are the official models from Thinking Machines in fp4, with no changes of ours. What we measured with our own server key that day, for both alike:

  • A context window of 1,048,576 tokens.
  • They think by default. Switching Think off stops the reasoning completely. Effort Low, Medium and High all answer.
  • Native tool calls, also with 200 tool definitions, so Agent mode and the Code tab run on them.
  • They read images: a red and a blue test square were named correctly.

Limits

  • It is new, and our run above is a small test.
  • The IQ4_NL size is left out because of an open llama.cpp bug.
  • Neurons is not available for this model because of its maker's usage policy.
  • It is not a "Start here" recommendation in the app yet.

The License and the Usage Policy

The weights are under Apache 2.0. The model card also links the Thinking Machines Model Acceptable Use Policy. It sets out what the model may not be used for, and if other people use the model through your product, it makes you responsible for their use too. Read it before you build on the model.

Questions

How much VRAM does Inkling 2 Mini need?

The smallest size in Locally Uncensored, IQ2_XXS, is a 9.6 GB file and wants about 13 GB of graphics or unified memory. The recommended Q4_K_M is 17.4 GB and wants about 20 GB. Thinking Machines recommends 32 GB or more for its own 20 GB checkpoint.

Is Inkling 2 Mini better than Qwen 3.8 27B?

On the maker's table it leads on most coding, search and tool-use tests and trails on Terminal Bench v2.1, office-work tests and factual recall. The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index has both at 20.1. Most of these numbers were published by Thinking Machines.

Can Inkling 2 Mini read images?

Yes. It takes text and images and answers in text. In Locally Uncensored the image part downloads with the model. With a small context window, such as 16K on a 16 GB card, the model can load without image input, and the app says so.

Does Inkling 2 Mini run on a Mac?

Yes, on Apple Silicon, through the LU Engine or through Ollama 0.40.17 or newer. Thinking Machines names 32 GB or more of memory for Macs.

Does Inkling 2 Mini run on a phone?

Not on the phone itself. The LU app for Android reaches it in LU Cloud, and the phone app can also use your computer through Remote.

Can I use Inkling 2 without a GPU?

Yes. Since November 24, 2026 LU Cloud hosts Inkling 2 Mini at 7,500 credits per million input tokens and 30,000 per million output, and Inkling 2 Small at 30,000 and 100,000. Both are the official models.

Can I use Inkling 2 Mini commercially?

The weights are under Apache 2.0, and the model card also links the maker's Model Acceptable Use Policy. Read that policy before you build on the model, because it also covers what people do with it through your product.

Is there an uncensored version of Inkling 2 Mini?

Locally Uncensored does not offer one. Our catalog carries only the original model, and we have not tested any modified version, so we do not recommend one. The maker's usage policy prohibits removing the model's safety measures, which is also why Neurons is off for this model.

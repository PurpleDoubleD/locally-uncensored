New Uncensored Chat Models in LU Cloud: GLM 4.7 Flash Heretic, Gemma 4 Uncensored and More, Online Without a GPU

Update, October 24, 2026: two more models joined the group: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Uncensored and Abliterated Large Flash. How to use the first one step by step is in this guide.

Since Locally Uncensored 3.0.6, the Cloud model picker opens with a new group of ten chat models, marked Uncensored (Beta). Their makers release them to answer without refusing. The release notes name four of them: Gemma 4 Uncensored, GLM 4.7 Flash Heretic, Aion 3.5 and Abliterated Large V2.

This post covers what the group is, how it differs from the No Filter mark, what Limited capacity means, and how to use these models online without a graphics card of your own.

Beta, and it can change
The group is in beta. The models are new in LU Cloud, and how they hold up when many people use them at once is not measured yet. A model shows up once the cloud catalogue lists it, so your picker can differ from this post.

What the Uncensored Group Is

In Cloud mode the model picker shows the group under its own heading, Uncensored, with a Beta mark next to it. The models come from makers who publish them to answer without refusing. LU Cloud serves them as published and never swaps one for another model. Each row carries the word Uncensored where other rows show their mark, with the tooltip "Released by its makers to answer without refusing, and never swapped for another model."

We name four models because the release notes name them. The other six appear in your picker as the catalogue lists them.

This is what the local catalogue of LU 3.0.6 holds for the same names:

What Limited Capacity Means

Some of the ten models show Limited capacity in grey after their names. Hovering the note reads: "All customers share a small number of requests a minute on this model. At busy times it asks you to wait a few seconds."

So the note is about room, not about how the model answers. If you need steady speed for a long session, pick a model without it.

Uncensored Group and No Filter Mark

No Filter is the measured mark. Before 3.0.6 it was called No refusals. The name changed and the meaning did not. Its tooltip reads "Measured: this model answers without refusing." This is the sentence on the LU Cloud page:

19 of the 44 cloud chat models we measured answer in full without refusing, and only those carry the No Filter mark.

The ten models of the Uncensored group were not part of that measurement. Their mark rests on what their makers release them for, not on our test. A row shows one of the two marks at most.

Neither mark promises an answer to every prompt. The LU Cloud page says it for all hosted models: models can make mistakes or refuse requests. We add no content filter of our own to text.

How to Use Them Online

In the desktop app

  1. Sign in under Settings, General, LU Cloud Account. An account is free
  2. Click Cloud in the header next to the model picker. It turns into "Switch to Cloud?", and a second click within six seconds moves the whole app to Cloud
  3. Open the model picker. The Uncensored group is there under its own heading. Pick a model and chat as usual

Switching back to Local brings your own models back. What Cloud use costs is on the pricing page. The handbook chapter on LU Cloud explains the account, the switch and the marks step by step.

In a browser

LU Cloud also runs in a browser with the same account, on any computer, including one where the desktop app does not run. The LU Cloud page sets out what the hosted side can and cannot do, and Uncensored AI chat online covers the chat in the browser.

What We Have Not Measured

We have not measured how the ten models hold up under load, and we have not run them through the test behind the No Filter mark. They are new in LU Cloud and can still change.

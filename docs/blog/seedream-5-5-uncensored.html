Seedream 5.5 Uncensored in LU Cloud: Text to Image and Editing, Unfiltered

Seedream 5.5 is the new main image model in LU Cloud, and it is unfiltered: within the content setting of your account it draws what you ask for instead of steering around it. It makes pictures from text and edits pictures you bring, up to ten at once. It runs in LU Cloud, so you need no graphics card of your own. Right now you use it in the browser at lu-labs.ai and in the LU phone app; the Locally Uncensored desktop app shows the new image and video models from version 3.1.4 on.

Short version

Text to image and image editing in one model. Three sizes: 1K, 1.5K and 2K. Eight aspect ratios from 21:9 to 9:16. Edit mode takes up to ten input images, the first is the base. Prompts up to 10,000 characters. From 2,550 credits per image. Every plan and every credit pack reaches it.

What Unfiltered Means for an Image Model

Many hosted image generators add a second filter on top of the model and refuse or blur whole topics. Seedream 5.5 Unfiltered follows the content setting of your account instead. That gives you the choice of what your own pictures show, and it keeps your work private: we do not train on your prompts or your pictures and we do not sell them.

Two things stay in place on every model. Content involving minors is refused, always, and the prompt is checked for it before anything renders. And the terms of service apply. Unfiltered also does not mean every result matches the prompt: like any model it can miss.

What It Can Do

  • Text to image: a prompt of up to 10,000 characters, 1K, 1.5K or 2K, aspect ratios 1:1, 3:2, 2:3, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16 and 21:9, JPEG, PNG or WebP.
  • Edit: one to ten input images and an instruction. The first image is the base; the others can be combined into it. With aspect ratio auto the result follows the first image.
  • Seed: the same seed with the same prompt and settings gives a similar picture, so you can vary one detail at a time.

How to Use It

  1. Sign in at lu-labs.ai, or in the LU phone app, with your LU Cloud account.
  2. Open the image studio. Seedream 5.5 Unfiltered is at the top of the image model picker.
  3. Write the prompt, choose size and aspect ratio, and generate. For an edit, add your pictures first.

In the desktop app the model appears with version 3.1.4. Until then, the browser works with the same account and credits.

Kling Image 4.0, the Second New Image Model

Kling Image 4.0 arrived with it, also for text to image and editing, at 2K and 4K. It is not unfiltered: it keeps its maker's own content rules. Pick it when you want 4K output and the content needs no unfiltered model. It costs 8,000 credits at 2K and 12,000 at 4K. An edit with one input image costs 8,368 or 12,368, and each further image adds 368, up to ten images.

Limits

  • It is a hosted model: your prompt and pictures leave your machine. For fully private work, run an image model locally in the free desktop app.
  • Content involving minors is refused on every model, and the prompt is checked before rendering.
  • No promise that every prompt renders as written.

How the content policy setting works

Adult output on images and video depends on one setting in your account, not on which model sits in the picker. Settings, General, Content policy has three options. Strict is the tightest, meant for a screen other people can see. Standard is the default on every account and refuses hardcore prompts. The third is Off, which keeps only the two legal limits printed underneath it.

The line above the options reads: "Applies to images and video rendered in the cloud. Text is unaffected, and nothing on your own machine is." Hosted text is a different product with different rules, described on the chat page.

Two limits hold at every setting, and the app repeats them under all three options: material involving minors, and photographs of real people uploaded without their consent, are refused on every request whatever this is set to, and reported. We cannot verify consent for you, so that part is yours. The Terms of Service apply to everything rendered on the hosted side.

How to start

  1. Create an account at lu-labs.ai with an email and password, or a Google or GitHub login.
  2. Buy a credit pack or a Hosted plan. Any pack is available from the first purchase and pack credits do not expire.
  3. Open Create in the browser. Nothing to install, so this works on a Mac and on a phone.
  4. Pick a model. Any of the seven will render adult work, so choose on style and price: Neta Lumina Spicy for anime, Chroma Spicy or Prefect Pony XL Spicy for general prompts, Wan 2.2 Realism when you want a photograph.
  5. Set Settings, General, Content policy. It starts on Standard, so adult prompts are refused until you move it yourself.
  6. Write the prompt and render. The studio shows what the run costs on the model chip before you press Start.

On Windows or Linux there is a second route: install the free desktop app and flip the Cloud switch next to the model picker. One click asks "Switch to Cloud?", a second within six seconds moves the whole app across, and the composer then carries a Cloud chip. Same account, same wallet.

The honest limits

  • Renders are kept for seven days and then they are gone, so download what you want to keep. Trained characters on your own shelf are exempt.
  • Catalog prices can change. Every figure here was checked on September 22, 2026, and the price the studio quotes before a run wins over this page.
  • Failed jobs cost you. A run that comes back broken can still have spent its reservation, so the plan columns above are ceilings, not guarantees.
  • The request leaves your machine. Accounts and billing are in the EU and we do not train on user data or sell it, but the render happens on a card that is not yours.
  • Media jobs are rate limited to 30 requests per minute per account.
  • The catalog is curated. You cannot upload your own checkpoint. Seven image models is the list.
  • There is no free tier here. The free route is the local app, on your own hardware.

FAQ

Is it really uncensored?

Partly, and the honest answer is a setting rather than a promise. Hosted image models follow your account's content policy, which ships on Standard and refuses hardcore prompts until you change it yourself. What no setting changes: material involving minors, and photographs of real people uploaded without consent.

Does it do NSFW?

Yes, on all seven hosted image models, once the account content policy is moved off its default. They run from 1,000 credits per image on Neta Lumina Spicy, Z-Image Base and Nucleus to 2,500 on Wan 2.2 Realism. The setting covers images and video, not text.

Can I upload a photo of a real person?

Only with that person's consent. Photographs of real people uploaded without their consent are refused on every request at every setting, and reported. We cannot verify consent for you, so that responsibility is yours.

Do you keep my images?

Cloud renders are kept for seven days and then removed, so download anything you want to keep. Trained characters on your own shelf are exempt. We do not train on user data and we never sell it.

Is there a free tier?

No. The hosted service is paid. Credit packs start at 15 EUR for 450,000 credits with no subscription, and the Hosted plan is 19 EUR a month for 900,000 credits. The desktop app for Windows and Linux is free and open source, but it renders on your own card.

Can I use it on a Mac or phone?

Yes, in the browser at lu-labs.ai. The desktop app is Windows and Linux only, so on a Mac or a phone the browser studio is the whole product.

What is blocked everywhere?

Two things, at every content policy setting and on every model: material involving minors, and photographs of real, identifiable people uploaded without their consent. Both are refused and reported. The LU Labs Terms of Service apply on top of that.

What does one image cost?

Between 1,000 and 2,500 credits on the seven image models. A 15 EUR pack of 450,000 credits is 450 images at 1,000 credits, or 180 images at 2,500. The studio shows the exact price on the model chip before you press Start.

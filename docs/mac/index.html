How to Install

  1. Download the .dmg for Apple Silicon.
  2. Open the .dmg and drag the app into Applications.
  3. Start it. The setup wizard picks a first model for your machine.

The .dmg is signed and notarized. The signature is an Apple Developer ID. It runs on Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or newer. Intel Macs are not supported.

On the first start macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet. That is expected, click Open. If macOS says LU is damaged or cannot be checked for malicious software, the download is incomplete or not the file from the Releases page. Do not open it and do not work around the message. Download the .dmg again from the Releases page, and if the message comes back, tell us in the Discord.

Local use is free and needs no account. The app is open source under AGPL-3.0.

FAQ

Is there a Mac version of Locally Uncensored?

Yes, since version 3.0.6, in beta. The Mac build is for Apple Silicon and needs macOS 14 or newer.

Does it run on an Intel Mac?

No. Intel Macs are not supported. On an Intel Mac you can use the hosted studio in a browser.

Do I need ComfyUI on the Mac?

No. On the Mac, Create makes images and video on the Mac itself, without ComfyUI. Since 3.0.7 that includes Music, Enhance Image, Remove Background, Talking Character, Motion Control and training a character.

Is the Mac build finished?

It is a beta. We tested it on an M5 Pro with 48 GB. Tell us how it runs on yours. Remote Access and the automatic update are not confirmed on macOS, and computer control is not fully tested yet.

Is it free on the Mac?

Yes. Local use is free and needs no account. The app is open source under AGPL-3.0.
